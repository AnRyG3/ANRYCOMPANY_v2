# ANRYCAMPANY Final Checklist

Use this file before saying a video is complete.

1. Confirm the final video path exists.
2. Confirm 1080x1920 vertical format when possible.
3. Confirm audio stream is present.
4. Confirm duration is reasonable.
5. Confirm no removed phrases remain in script or generated metadata.
6. For new videos, confirm `production_manifest.md` and the actual telop frames map one-to-one in the same cut order.
7. Confirm actual source materials exclude rejected, archived, CapCut-export, `codex_generated_images`, and reference-only folders.
8. Confirm telop approval occurred before audio/video generation and the approved CTA remains in final telop and narration.
9. Confirm voice reading rules were applied to narration text.
10. Confirm frame count matches narration segment count unless intentionally different.
11. Confirm posting title, description, hashtags, final path, schedule, and URL are recorded when provided.

Report only meaningful final facts to the user: what changed, where it is, and what was checked.
